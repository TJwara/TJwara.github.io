INTEGRATED ACCOUNTING PLATFORM v0.1.4

1. Extract the complete ZIP to a new folder.
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
3. Confirm that the sidebar displays v0.1.4.
4. Open Administration, select Add company, and enter the legal company name.
5. The company code is generated automatically. Select Create and Prepare Company.
6. To edit or delete records, open the relevant register and use Edit or Delete on that row.
7. To clear a company's records or delete the company, open Administration > Companies.

The training guide, blank import template and completed sample workbook are in the training-pack folder.

Do not run the platform from inside the ZIP. Extract the ZIP first.
