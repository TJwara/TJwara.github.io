Integrated Accounting Platform

The legal terms, privacy provisions, data-processing rules, disclaimers and related notices governing use of the platform.

1. Legal Overview

The Integrated Accounting Platform is a business software environment designed to bring Cost & Managerial Accounting and Financial Accounting into a connected platform using common business data. It may provide dashboards, cost analysis, financial records, management information, reporting, data imports, exports and related functionality.

These policies apply to the platform, its web interfaces, software functionality, documentation and related online services unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

By accessing or using the platform, you acknowledge that you have had a reasonable opportunity to read these terms and agree to comply with them.

You must not misuse the platform, interfere with its operation, attempt to gain unauthorised access, circumvent security controls, introduce malicious code, unlawfully copy or reverse engineer protected software, or use the platform to process information in violation of applicable law or another person's rights.
